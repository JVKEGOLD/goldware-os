GoldWare Voice

Two keys. Right Option types what you say into whatever app you are in. Right Command hands what you say to GoldWare, who files it as a task or a note. The speech model, the language model, and every recording stay on this Mac.

1

Click into any text box.

Mail, Notes, a browser field, a chat. Wherever the cursor is, the text goes.

2

Hold Right Option and talk.

The Option key to the right of the space bar. The pill at the bottom of the screen shows the orb and your words as you say them.

3

Let go.

About a second later the cleaned text is pasted in. Your clipboard is put back the way it was.

Saying things to GoldWare

Hold Right Command and talk the way you would to a person. It never sends a message or finishes a task for you.
Task

“Remind me Friday to send the invoice.”

Becomes Send the invoice, due Friday, in your Tasks list.

Note

“Idea: add a weekly summary email.”

Filed as a note with your exact words attached, so you can decide later what it becomes.

Snippet

“Paste my address.”

Inserts a saved snippet into the app in front. Nothing is pasted unless you ask for it.

Days it understands
“Thursday”, “tomorrow”, and “end of next week” become real due dates. Say “urgent” to mark a task high priority.
Snippets
Saved text such as an address or a link, kept only on this Mac. The model sees their names, never their values. Keep passwords and card numbers in a password manager instead.
Terminal commands
Say “Let’s work” to open a terminal in each corner (what they run is set by letsWork in goldware.json). “Finish up” asks every Hermes to wrap up. “Lock up” closes idle terminals and keeps any agent that is mid-task. “Clear out” closes only the Hermes terminals you have not written in. The whole clip has to be the phrase, so a task that mentions them stays a task.

What happens when you let go

Every step runs on this Mac. Nothing is sent to a cloud service.
  1. RecordMicrophone16 kHz audio while the key is held
  2. TranscribeLocal Whisperwhisper.cpp, about a third of a second a sentence
  3. Clean upLocal modelRuns in Ollama, set by models.local
  4. PasteInto the app in frontClipboard plus Cmd+V, then restored
  5. RememberLocal historyRaw words, final text, app, timings
The cleanup model
Drops um, uh, and false starts, keeps only the fix when you correct yourself, and obeys “new line”, “comma”, and “question mark”. It never answers what you say: a dictated question is pasted as a question. Very short phrases skip cleanup. Switch it off in the menu to paste exactly what Whisper heard.
History
Every dictation is stored with what was heard next to what was pasted, so you can judge the cleanup. The menu bar lists your last few, and clicking one copies it again if a paste missed.
The orb
A tiny breathing orb rests at the bottom of the screen. It rolls while it listens, sweeps while it transcribes, wires up while the assistant thinks, and settles into a slow ring when it is done. Gold means the assistant is working. Drag it anywhere.
Agents on the pill
Your Office agents sit beside the orb as their pixel characters, each with a small mark: a hopping gold ? when it has a question, a spinning ring while it works, a green dot when it is ready. Hover one and it lifts with a soft gold glow (its name and task show as a tooltip); click it to bring its terminal forward. Hover the orb itself for your recent dictations, where the highlight glides from row to row.
The shelf
Start dragging a file or screenshot and the pill grows into a “Drop here” target. Drop it and it stays as a thumbnail you can drag back out into any app or folder later. Click a thumbnail to open it.

Private by design

It runs on this Mac.
Nothing leaves
Speech recognition and the language model both run locally. Recordings, words, and history stay in the app data folder and out of Git.
Permissions
Microphone for hearing you, and Accessibility for pasting and for the right-hand keys.

If something is off

Nothing happens when I hold the key

Check that the app is in the menu bar, and that System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility has it switched on. Make sure it is the right Option key.

It says it did not hear anything

Taps under half a second and near-silent clips are ignored on purpose, because speech models invent text from silence. Hold the key a beat before you start talking.

The text did not paste

Some fields, like password boxes, block pasting. The dictation is still saved: open it from the menu bar history and click to copy.

The first dictation was slow

The cleanup model loads into memory the first time and stays loaded for a while. The first run after a long break can take a few seconds.

GoldWare Vision

Vision turns the camera above the screen into a mirror, a scanner, and a hands-free pointer. Every frame is read on this Mac and dropped. No video, photo, or scan image is ever saved or sent.

1

Turn it on.

Press ⌘ ⌥ together and let go, or say “turn on Vision Mode” while holding Right Command.

2

Unlock with both hands.

Nothing moves until you give your two-hand unlock gesture. The camera preview starts hidden; the gold lock right of the notch shows locked or unlocked. Once unlocked, an OK sign shows the preview.

3

Use your hand.

Point to move, pinch to click, pinch and move to scroll. Held gestures take about a second, so a hand passing by never acts.

Mirror and scan

No unlock needed. The camera runs only while the mirror is open.
🪞
Hand mirror

Rest the pointer behind the camera notch.

A live, mirrored view slides down with dots on your fingertips. Move away and it folds back up.

Scan a document

Hold a card, receipt, or page still.

A gold outline locks on and closes as you hold. The text is read on this Mac.

Thumbs up files it as a task

Hold a thumbs up.

One task in your Tasks list with what it is, any due date, and the exact details. Nothing is sent to anyone.

Vision Mode pointer

Move and click with your hand. Needs unlocking first.
Point

Index finger out, move it.

Moves the pointer like a finger on a trackpad.

Pinch to click

Thumb to index finger.

Pinch and let go. The pointer holds still a moment so the click lands where you aimed. Twice quickly double-clicks.

Pinch and move to scroll

Pinch, hold, and move.

The page follows your hand, any direction. Let go mid-move to fling it; pinch again to catch it.

Open hand or fist rests

Nothing happens.

A safe place to rest while you reposition, like lifting a mouse.

How a scan becomes a task

Every step runs on this Mac.
  1. FindDocument detectionApple Vision finds the page in the frame
  2. StillOne straightened frameCropped and flattened, kept in memory only
  3. ReadOn-device textExact spelling, numbers, and emails
  4. DecideLocal modelPicks what it is and what you need to do
  5. FileTasksOnly after your thumbs up

Privacy and the camera light

When the camera is on
Only while the mirror is open or Vision Mode is on. The green light always matches something you can see.
Nothing is recorded
Frames are read for hands and documents in memory and dropped.
Face ID (optional)
Off by default. Turn it on in Vision Settings and look at the camera for 8 seconds; after that only hands that belong to your face drive. Only a 128-number face fingerprint is kept, on this Mac.
Permissions
Camera access for the mirror and scanning. Vision Mode also uses Accessibility access, to move the pointer and click.

If something is off

My hand does nothing

Vision Mode starts locked. Check the gold lock right of the notch (closed means locked) and give your two-hand unlock gesture with both hands in view of the camera.

The mirror does not open

Rest the pointer at the very top of the screen, inside the notch, for a moment. On a display without a notch, use the top center.

Clicks happen by accident

Keep your thumb apart from your index finger while you aim, and open your hand to rest.

It will not lock onto my card

Hold it closer so it fills more of the frame, against a darker background, and keep it still.
